## Design Context

See `PRODUCT.md` for full product context. Key points:

- **Register:** product (a tool, not a marketing surface) — design serves the trading workflow.
- **Personality:** precise, disciplined, professional. Bloomberg Terminal-style trading desk tool — dark theme, cyan accent, data-dense, monospace for numbers.
- **Avoid:** the generic SaaS dashboard cliché (gradient cards, rounded-everything pastel admin panels, decorative motion).
- **Accessibility:** P&L/win-loss red-green coloring must be paired with +/− signs or labels, not color alone.
